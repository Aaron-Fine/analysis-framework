# Ledger closeout review — 6 November 2026

## Decision

The experiment is being stopped. It was not predictive enough to justify
continuing. The ledger is closed and framework 0.3 is final. No probability was
revised at closure, and no resolution rule was amended.

## Closing rules applied

- **Past cutoff:** Iran (31 October). Resolved under its original rules and
  scored.
- **Before cutoff:** Greenland arrangement, Greenland posture, tariffs, and the
  administrative state. All four were withdrawn unscored on the same date. That
  date was chosen by the decision to stop, not by evidence on any case, which
  limits selective withdrawal.
- **Closing-status notes:** each withdrawn case gets a dated note summarizing
  the latest evidence. The notes are not outcome vectors.
- **Likely misses:** where an outcome now looks likely to defeat the leading
  branch (the Greenland arrangement), the conditional score is stated in the
  note but not recorded.

## Iran resolution

The outcome is **prolonged regional attrition**. Each higher-precedence test
fails:

- **Regime fracture:** no loss of command over the regular forces or the IRGC
  was found.
- **Major escalation:** no ground campaign or occupation, and no nuclear or
  radiological event.
- **Settlement:** no agreement was operative on 31 October. Doha mediation was
  still at the proposal-review stage, and 3–4 November reporting describes no
  ceasefire.
- **Armistice or frozen conflict:** no agreement existed, and October was not
  strike-free. Ships were hit by mines in mid-October, at least 12 attacks were
  reported in the week to 16 October, a tanker was mined on 29 October, and
  Houthi–Saudi exchanges took place on 10–13 and 25 October.

Brier score of the last public forecast (55/15/6/15/6/3, 9 September): **0.2556**.
The same outcome scores 0.3472 against the 18 August forecast and 0.5628 against
the 2 August baseline.

## Withdrawn cases: closing evidence

- **Greenland arrangement**
  - Inatsisartut approved the agreement on 3 November: 16 for, 0 against,
    7 blank.
  - The Folketing first reading is set for 12 November. A final vote has been
    reported for about 19 November, but that date is unconfirmed.
  - No Article XII note had been reported, so the agreement was not in force at
    closure.
  - If it takes effect by 31 December, it resolves to the 28% top-level-framework
    branch. That would score 0.7458, and the 36% leading branch would miss.
- **Greenland posture:** none of the operational tests was met.
  - A Seabee technical survey at Narsarsuaq. Surveys are excluded.
  - Pentagon "planning cycle" remarks on 20 October.
  - No awards found for the new sites.
- **Tariffs:** the general rate is below 20%. The headline Section 301 rates are
  10% and 12.5%, and effective-rate estimates run from about 7% to 11%.
  - Coverage is 99.4% if measured by country scope, which fits the broad floor.
  - It is roughly 50–55% if measured by import value actually subject to added
    duties (Brookings and Tax Foundation estimates), which fits the narrower
    regime.
  - The denominator was never defined. It was flagged on 2 October and
    deliberately not repaired.
  - The trade-court ruling on the Section 301 action was still pending.
- **Administrative state**
  - About 8,000 employees in more than 4,800 positions were placed in Schedule
    Policy/Career. No injunction was found.
  - *Trump v. Slaughter* overruled *Humphrey's Executor*; *Trump v. Cook*
    preserved Federal Reserve process.
  - Staffing strain is documented at the IRS, the VA, and the FAA, with advocacy
    and press reports on SSA and NWS.
  - The capacity test has no duration or comparator, and the strain traces
    mainly to headcount cuts rather than to the new schedule.

## Limits of the closeout research

Evidence came from a limited public-source web search. Many pages could not be
fetched in full from the review environment, so several facts rest on search
summaries and secondary reporting. Spot checks confirmed the mid-October Hormuz
mine strikes, the competing claims to control of the strait (Al Jazeera,
17 October), and Greenland's 3 November parliamentary approval.

Negative findings are limited-search findings: no regime fracture, no ground
campaign, no operative agreement, and no Schedule Policy/Career removals. The
rule-1 and rule-2 negatives for Iran are robust, because events of that size
would be widely reported.

## Final product

`public/closeout/` contains the final assessment:

- the record
- why the framework was not predictive
- lens-by-lens notes on where it adds explanatory power over an ordinary
  baseline
- a use/avoid table and a five-step procedure for reuse
- what a valid predictive test would have required
- loose-end dates for readers

Closing notes and navigation links were also added to the introduction,
framework, research, and 404 pages, the sitemap, `llms.txt`, and the README.
