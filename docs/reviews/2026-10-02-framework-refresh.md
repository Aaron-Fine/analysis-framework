# Framework 0.3 review — 2 October 2026

## Changes and rationale

Parsimony is now an explicit entry rule, stopping rule, and forecast-card
requirement. Begin with an ordinary baseline, identify each mechanism's
incremental contribution, and remove assumptions that do not distinguish
outcomes. Relevant evidence and counterpart agency still belong in the analysis.
The four lenses are not independent probability weights.

The commitment ladder is now an evidence checklist rather than an inevitable
sequence. The publication distinguishes explanation, prediction, and validation,
and distinguishes a thesis confirmation from resolution of a scored question.
New forecasts should publish baseline and framework distributions together;
historical baseline probabilities cannot be supplied retrospectively.

Older research annotations that proposed additional lenses or layers now map
their findings to the four existing questions and required audits. Bibliographic
dates distinguish journal publication from a later arXiv posting. The ledger's
structured-data name no longer implies that there are only four cases.

## Greenland adjudication

The access-without-annexation thesis is confirmed at signature. Under the
unaltered governing-arrangement rules, the signed text fits the 28% top-level
framework branch, not the 31% site-specific branch or the leading 36% no-new-
arrangement branch. The question remains open because it requires an operative
arrangement at 23:59 UTC on 31 December 2026. No score is assigned now.

The operational question remains separate. Its judgmental distribution changes
from 45/30/20/5 to 30/25/30/15; the signed named-site provision changes the
prospects for additional-site use but does not itself satisfy an implementation
test. The original baseline and operational criteria are preserved.

## Research refresh scope

Six additions cover explanation versus prediction (Shmueli), conservative
forecasting (Graefe, Green, and Armstrong), signaling (Fearon), proper scoring
(Gneiting and Raftery), hybrid forecasting (Benjamin et al.), and forecasting-agent
judgment (Liptay et al.). Linked primary papers or publisher records were retrieved;
the public annotations state their scope and limitations. The earlier map is
retained and is not represented as a freshly audited systematic review.

## Remaining work

Iran, tariff, and administrative-state evidence and probabilities were not
refreshed. Their displayed as-of dates remain authoritative. Legacy measurement
gaps include the tariff coverage denominator/general-rate estimator and the
duration/comparator for capacity degradation. Amendments should be dated and
original rules retained. The small, dependent ledger cannot yet demonstrate
calibration or incremental skill over a baseline.
