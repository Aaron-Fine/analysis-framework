Iran conflict and negotiations

As of
2 August 2026
Horizon
31 October 2026 · medium/long
Decision unit
Trump and a small negotiating group; implementation requires multiple states

Question: What political-military condition will characterize the U.S.–Iran conflict at the resolution date? The 1 August pause and outline of a deal raise the armistice branch, but repeated failed pauses make a durable settlement much less likely than a near-term interruption in strikes.

34%Prolonged regional attrition
29%Armed armistice / frozen conflict
17%U.S.-favored coercive settlement
9%Major ground, territorial, or nuclear escalation
6%Iran-favored coercive settlement
5%Regime fracture / loss of central control

Dominant · Ends: preventing an Iranian nuclear capability and reopening Hormuz appear relatively fixed; timing and settlement terms remain negotiable.

Dominant · Feedback: U.S. strike capacity creates pressure while Iran returns costs through shipping, Gulf infrastructure, energy prices, casualties, and regional bargaining.

Supporting · Authority: Trump can pause or resume attacks quickly, but Israel, Iran, Gulf governments, armed forces, and inspectors determine durability.

Resolution and update triggers

  • Armistice: at least 30 consecutive strike-free days plus routine tanker passage and an operating negotiation channel.
  • Attrition: recurring strike or maritime-disruption cycles without a durable settlement process.
  • Classification: use the condition at the deadline; regime fracture and major escalation take precedence over settlement or attrition, and a qualifying armistice takes precedence over attrition.
  • Move ten points: a signed verification mechanism, sustained Hormuz reopening, territorial seizure, major U.S. casualties, outside-navy combat, or visible Iranian command fracture.

Water-system cyberattacks

As of
9 August 2026
Horizon
16 August 2026 · short
Decision unit
Utilities and technical agencies for containment; Trump for attribution and escalation

Question: How will the federal response be publicly characterized by the resolution date? Reporting now places related incidents in at least 12 states, and federal investigators continue to suspect Iranian-linked actors. But no formal public attribution has been made, reported drinking water remains safe, and no new serious effect has forced presidential escalation. Broader scope modestly raises the Iranian-affiliation branch; elapsed time lowers the escalation branch.

55%No public high-confidence Iranian attribution; no response beyond limited measures or technical containment
30%Iranian affiliation publicly established; no more than a limited acknowledged response
10%Presidential escalation beyond limited cyber, financial, law-enforcement, or diplomatic measures
5%Credible evidence materially redirects public attribution away from Iran

Dominant · Authority: the state can remediate exposed PLCs while the president disputes or politicizes attribution.

Dominant · Commitment: advisories, manual operation, device isolation, and forensic work are implemented; retaliation remains reversible talk or covert action.

Supporting · Feedback: localized, recoverable effects permit blame and costs to remain distributed; contamination or casualties would rapidly concentrate exposure.

Resolution and update triggers

  • Qualified attribution: no high-confidence public federal attribution by the deadline.
  • Iranian affiliation: a named federal agency, public intelligence-community assessment, charging document, or sanctions finding attributes the campaign to Iran, the IRGC, or an Iranian-directed or affiliated group. Anonymous-official suspicion does not qualify.
  • Limited response: an acknowledged indictment, sanction, disruption, or diplomatic action without a kinetic strike justified by the cyberattack.
  • Escalation: a kinetic action or another acknowledged response beyond the limited-response category, explicitly justified by this campaign. Technical containment is not retaliation.
  • Classification: first classify any qualifying escalation; otherwise credible redirection away from Iran; otherwise public establishment of Iranian affiliation; all remaining outcomes resolve as qualified or no attribution. Use the strongest qualifying public attribution and acknowledged response by the deadline.
  • Move ten points: chemical-dosing manipulation, prolonged water loss, deaths, official intelligence attribution, a credible claim of responsibility, or activity beyond the identified device pattern.

FBI and EPA: operational disruptions and mitigations
CBS News: incidents reported in at least 12 states; no formal attribution

Post-court tariff regime

As of
2 August 2026
Horizon
20 January 2027 · long
Decision unit
Trump and USTR, constrained by courts, customs administration, markets, and counterparties

Question: How broad will the operative U.S. tariff regime be at the resolution date? The Supreme Court removed one authority, but the administration rebuilt a broad floor under other statutes.

50%Broad floor covers at least 75% of import value; general rate stays below 20%
25%Narrower sector/country regime covers 25–75% of import value
15%Material rollback leaves duties covering less than 25% of import value
10%Expanded regime retains broad coverage with a general rate of 20% or more

Dominant · Ends: the tariff direction appears more fixed than any legal basis, schedule, exemption, or stated justification.

Dominant · Commitment: replacement authorities and continuous collection indicate an implemented program rather than a recurring trial balloon.

Supporting · Feedback: courts, bond and equity markets, consumer prices, foreign concessions, and concentrated lobbying are more likely to change exemptions than direction.

Resolution and update triggers

  • Classification: use the latest authoritative estimate of import value subject to additional Trump-era duties. The 20%-or-more general-rate branch takes precedence over the other broad-coverage branch.
  • Move ten points: adverse final court rulings on Section 301 implementation, a large Treasury-market dislocation, sustained inflation acceleration attributed to tariffs, congressional restriction, or a negotiated multi-country rollback.
  • Patronage test: exemptions count as particularistic only when access or unequal treatment is documented, not merely because a sector benefits.

Reuters: Section 301 duties replace the temporary global tariff
Supreme Court: IEEPA does not authorize tariffs

Administrative-state restructuring

As of
2 August 2026
Horizon
20 January 2028 · horizon
Decision unit
White House and OPM; agencies, courts, unions, Congress, and labor markets determine implementation

Question: What condition best describes the Schedule Policy/Career program and presidential control of independent agencies at the resolution date?

35%Durable centralization with limited severe service failure
30%Hybrid adaptation: authority survives but implementation remains uneven
20%Centralization accompanied by significant multi-agency capacity degradation
10%Substantial judicial or congressional rollback
5%Largely symbolic implementation with little durable output change

Dominant · Authority: this directly tests whether personal supervision can be embedded in law, staffing, review, and repeatable organizational routines.

Dominant · Commitment: eligibility and executive orders matter less than completed transfers, removals, replacements, changed decisions, and persistence.

Supporting · Feedback: litigation, vacancies, recruitment, backlogs, technical failures, and coalition costs determine whether centralization remains politically affordable.

Resolution and update triggers

  • Durable centralization: a majority of the roughly 8,000 identified positions are transferred or otherwise subjected to the new removal regime, and independent-agency supervision remains operational after major litigation.
  • Capacity degradation: sustained vacancies, backlogs, missed statutory duties, or technical failures are documented across at least three major agencies and linked to staffing or politicization.
  • Classification: rollback takes precedence if the core authorities no longer operate; otherwise use implementation breadth and the capacity test, with significant degradation taking precedence over limited-failure centralization.
  • Move ten points: final Supreme Court rulings, appropriations restrictions, large-scale removals, agency-level implementation data, or repeated service failures.

White House: implementing Schedule Policy/Career
Reuters: job protections removed from roughly 8,000 policy-influencing positions
